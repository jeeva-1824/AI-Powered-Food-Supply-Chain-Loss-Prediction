AgroRoute AI

Smart logistics intelligence for rural food supply chains.

AgroRoute AI is designed to minimize supply chain loss for India’s rural agriculture networks. Our platform combines advanced machine learning, real-time weather insights, interactive route planning, and image-based spoilage signals to help logistics partners make faster, safer decisions.

Mission

Reduce perishability losses by empowering agri-logistics with predictive route intelligence.

Vision

Build the trusted SaaS platform for end-to-end rural cold chain optimization.

AI Risk Predictor

Predict spoilage risk and loss percentage from environmental and product data.

Mandi Network

Locate certified government agri markets and recommend safe stops.

  • Predictive supply chain health monitoring.
  • Automated route scoring and risk categorization.

  • Multilingual UI and modern SaaS dashboard design.
